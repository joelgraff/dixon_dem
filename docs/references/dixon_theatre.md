# Dixon Theatre ("The Dixon: Historic Theatre")

**Location:** corner of **Galena Avenue and 2nd Street**, downtown Dixon.

## IMPORTANT address correction
The brief lists **114 S. Hennepin Ave**. Every source found in research (Cinema Treasures, the theatre's own venue listings, Discover Dixon, Visit Lee County, Visit Northwest Illinois) consistently gives the address as **114 S. Galena Avenue, Dixon, IL 61021** — Galena Ave, not Hennepin Ave. This also matches the photographed corner (street sign reads "2nd Street," and the building sits at a Galena Ave corner per the reference photos). Recommend modelling it at its documented Galena Ave location/corner unless the user has a specific reason to prefer Hennepin Ave.

## History
- Built **1922** on the site of the earlier Dixon Opera House (destroyed by fire in 1920).
- Designed by Chicago architectural firm **N. S. Spencer & Son**, completed/opened **March 15, 1922**.
- **1,140 seats**, fireproof construction throughout: brick, terra cotta, concrete, and steel.
- Described in "Italian Renaissance" styling; contemporary accounts praised "rich colors, beautiful blends of tapestries and walls, a magnificent organ."
- Still operating today as a nonprofit historic performing-arts venue ("The Dixon: Historic Theatre").

## Footprint & heights (estimated from photos)
- Corner commercial-block building, **2–3 storeys** at the front (Galena Ave) facade, stepping up to a taller **windowless brick fly-tower/auditorium block** behind and to the side (visible along 2nd St) that reads as a large blank brick wall roughly **3–4 storeys equivalent (estimate 14–16 m)** with a tall brick chimney/vent stack at the rear.
- Front facade width along Galena Ave: **estimate 15–18 m** (narrow, typical single-parcel downtown storefront width).
- Side auditorium block along 2nd St: much longer, **estimate 30–35 m** deep.

## Massing breakdown (primitives)
1. A 2–3 storey rectangular "front-of-house" block facing Galena Ave, ornamented facade.
2. A tall, largely blank rectangular **auditorium/fly-tower box** behind and beside it (visible along 2nd St as a big flat brick wall — good canvas for weathering/graffiti-free painterly grime, or in-game murals).
3. A tall slender brick **chimney/vent stack** rising above the roofline at the rear corner.
4. A projecting rectangular **marquee/blade sign box** cantilevered over the sidewalk at the main entrance, roughly at 2nd-floor-sill height.

## Facade description (front, bay by bay)
- Upper floor(s): buff/tan brick with classical terra-cotta or cast-stone ornament — a decorative **pedimented or cartouche element** centered above the corner, flanked by tall arched or rectangular window openings in a regular rhythm (the reference photo shows roughly 5–6 evenly spaced window bays across the upper front).
- A projecting cornice/parapet caps the front block, with a shaped/scalloped parapet silhouette visible against the sky (slightly stepped, not flat).
- Ground floor: storefront-height glazing and doors along the Galena Ave frontage (largely infilled/painted over in the modern photo — original elaborate marquee canopy is gone; today's marquee is a smaller projecting blade sign, not a full sidewalk-spanning canopy).
- **Marquee/blade sign:** a rectangular box sign projecting from the building at the entrance, maroon/burgundy background (`#6B1F2A` estimate) with white text reading **"HISTORIC DIXON THEATRE"**, and a changeable-letter reader-board panel below listing current shows/times.

## Materials & real colors (hex estimates)
- Brick (upper facade): warm buff-tan, `#C4B49A` to `#A8967A` in shadow.
- Side/rear auditorium block brick: slightly darker, more utilitarian brown-tan brick, `#9A8468`.
- Marquee sign: maroon/burgundy `#6B1F2A` background, white lettering `#F5F2EA`.
- Roofline trim/cornice: cast stone or painted metal, light gray-cream `#D8D2C4`.
- Chimney: red-brown brick, `#7A4536`.

## Roof
Flat/low-slope behind a shaped parapet at the front; flat over the auditorium block, not visible from street level except the tall chimney/vent stack silhouette.

## Signage
- **Marquee/blade sign:** "HISTORIC DIXON THEATRE" in white on a maroon field, projecting perpendicular to the building over the sidewalk near the corner entrance.
- A changeable-letter panel below the marquee for showtimes (generic black-and-white plastic-letter reader board).

## Notable details worth modelling
- The pedimented/cartouche ornament above the corner entrance — the single richest decorative element on an otherwise plain brick box.
- Regular window-bay rhythm on the upper facade.
- The large blank brick side wall along 2nd St — in real life this now hosts a small public art installation (a peace-themed sculpture and mounted panels, including an actual salvaged Berlin Wall segment) near the sidewalk; optional set-dressing, not part of the core building form.
- Tall brick chimney at the rear corner — good vertical landmark accent visible from side streets.

## Surrounding context
Corner site facing an intersection with mast-arm traffic signals; adjacent mid-rise brick commercial/bank buildings along Galena Ave; ordinary downtown sidewalk with brick paver curb strip; on-street parking. The theatre reads as one of several attached commercial-block buildings along the row, distinguished mainly by its marquee and larger auditorium massing behind.

## Must-get-right (top 5 for recognizability)
1. Corner position at **Galena Ave & 2nd St**, tall brick chimney visible at the rear.
2. Maroon **"HISTORIC DIXON THEATRE" blade marquee** projecting over the sidewalk at the entrance.
3. Buff-tan brick front facade with a decorative pediment/cartouche centered above the corner.
4. Regular window-bay rhythm across the 2–3 storey front block.
5. Large plain brick auditorium block stepping up behind the front facade, visible along 2nd St.
