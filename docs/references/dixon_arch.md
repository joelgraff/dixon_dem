# Dixon Welcome Arch ("DIXON" Memorial Arch)

**Location:** Spans **Galena Avenue** (US-52) at roughly the 100 block, just south of the Galena Ave bridge/Rock River crossing and immediately north of the Lee County Courthouse block — a strong visual anchor at the north entrance to downtown.
**Also known as:** Dixon Memorial Arch / Victory Memorial Arch / War Memorial Arch.

## IMPORTANT material correction
The ART_DIRECTION brief calls this a "steel" arch. **Research does not support that for the current structure.** History:
- 1919: original wood-frame-and-beaverboard triumphal arch, built to welcome home Lee County WWI veterans (Co. C, 123rd Field Artillery).
- Rebuilt/reinforced multiple times: ~1949 (called "unsightly hazard"), rebuilt ~1949, widened/heightened ~1965 (when Galena Ave became 4 lanes), refurbished 1979.
- **1984–85: the arch was taken down and replaced with the current structure, built of fiberglass**, rededicated Veterans Day (Nov 11) 1985. **This fiberglass arch is what stands today** and what should be modelled — it is the "current one," matching the project's instruction to model actual present-day structures (as with the bridge).
- Visually it reads as a smooth painted tube, not visible rivets/plate steel — consistent with a fiberglass shell over a steel armature, not an exposed steel truss.

## Location / orientation
Full-span arch over Galena Ave, oriented east-west across the street (Galena Ave runs roughly north-south through downtown here), so the arch faces traffic approaching from north or south. Two square pier bases sit at the curbline on each side of the 4-lane road. A masonry retaining wall / low stone wall runs along the sidewalk near the piers (visible in photos, likely part of the courthouse-block landscaping).

## Dimensions (estimated from photos — compare to 4-lane road, pickup truck, streetlight)
- Clear span across the road: **estimate 18–20 m (60–65 ft)** — covers all 4 lanes plus curb.
- Apex height above roadway: **estimate 10–11 m (33–36 ft)** — a pickup truck passing under looks small relative to the opening.
- Arch tube/member cross-section: **estimate 0.9–1.1 m (3–3.5 ft) diameter**, uniform round profile, no taper.
- Pier bases: square in plan, **estimate 1.2 m × 1.2 m**, roughly 2–2.5 m tall, clad to look like stone/concrete piers.

## Massing breakdown (primitives)
1. Two square prism pier bases (one each side of the road).
2. A single continuous smooth round-section arch (torus/tube segment, roughly semicircular to slightly parabolic) spanning between the piers.
3. A raised, stepped rectangular signboard mounted at the crown/apex, proud of the arch's outer curve, carrying the letters.
4. Individual block letters "D I X O N" mounted on the signboard, each letter appears to have a routed/raised face with a neon tube outline.

## Facade / signage description
- Arch is painted **smooth matte white/off-white**.
- At the crown, a stepped signboard (like a small pediment) carries **"DIXON"** in bold serif/slab-serif block letters, dark maroon/burgundy-red faces (`#6B1F1F` estimate) with a **red neon tube outline** visible lit at dusk/night (neon reads bright red, `#FF2A2A` emissive).
- No other text on the arch.

## Materials & colors (hex estimates)
- Arch shell: white/off-white, `#F2F0EA` (sunlit) to `#D9D6CE` (shadow).
- Pier bases: lighter stone-gray/cream cladding, `#E4E0D6`.
- Letters: dark maroon-red face `#6B1F1F`, red neon glow `#FF2A2A` at night.

## Roof / structure type
N/A — open arch structure, no roof.

## Notable details worth modelling
- Ornate black cast-iron **lamp posts** at the base of each pier, double-globe luminaires, with hanging flower baskets in summer — these frame the arch nicely and are worth including.
- Mast-arm traffic signals just beyond the arch on the courthouse side.
- Brick paver strip along the curb/gutter line under the arch (seen in reference photos) contrasting with plain concrete sidewalk.

## Surrounding context
- Courthouse dome and mature street trees visible through/beside the arch looking south.
- A church steeple is visible in the background in one dusk reference photo, a couple blocks south — useful skyline silhouette reference.
- Modern brick storefront/office building immediately adjacent on one side of the road.
- Traffic: ordinary 4-lane urban arterial with center turn markings, on-street angle or parallel parking nearby, mast-arm signals (not span-wire) at the nearest intersection.

## Must-get-right (top 5 for recognizability)
1. Smooth **white arch** in one continuous curve spanning the full 4-lane street.
2. **"DIXON"** spelled in individual raised letters at the crown, dark red with a red neon outline.
3. Two square pier bases planted at the curb on each side of the road.
4. Positioned near/against the courthouse dome and downtown skyline (visible together in wide shots).
5. Flanking ornate black lamp posts with hanging flower baskets.
