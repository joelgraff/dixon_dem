# Dixon Story Spine

A simple through-line for the Sierra-style text parser adventure. The historical scenes are the plot. The player is a witness or transient (or their bloodline) who can intervene or miss things. The story ends with the full dossier of what Dixon became because of the choices.

## Player Role
A newcomer or transient who returns to Dixon at key moments (or whose descendants do). Not the hero of history — a witness whose small actions leave scars or bloodlines.

## Through-Line
"What holds Dixon together when it breaks?" Each major scene is a rupture. The player’s actions either leave a scar or a bloodline that shows up later. No single protagonist spans 130 years.

## Scene Sequence

1. **1830 Ogee ferry / Dixon settling** — Arrive broke. Earn the timber plot or the ferry. Meet Dixon. Only pure "start from nothing" beat. Threads: freight hauling, land purchase, militia/Eli curiosity.

2. **1843 Joseph Smith arrest** — Agency test. Side with the Missouri officers, warn Smith, or stay out. Dossier shows what that cost or preserved. Load-bearing for later Mormon presence or absence.

3. **1873 Truesdell Bridge collapse** — Crisis state. Rescue someone (or fail to). That survivor’s line becomes a recurring name (real prototype: Goble/Gertie/Wadsworth). Named NPCs from historical record.

4. **1905 Billy Sunday revival** — Load-bearing reconvergence. Same tent, different faces depending on prior forks. Quiet absences (missing surnames, unbuilt storefronts) matter more than spectacle.

5. **1927 Lowell Park / Reagan lifeguard years** — Reflection scene. Not a puzzle. Final dossier lists what changed because of you (or because you didn’t act).

## Optional Side Threads
Insert as they open after a prior scene closes:
- Walgreens founding (civic growth)
- Sterling-Dixon Interurban (transport)
- 1968 druggist murder (Joseph Villiger / Benjamin Rippeon / Tom Wadsworth) — aftermath test
- Black Hawk War / Fort Dixon (1832), Bloody Gulch (1885), Dixon High School milestones

## Rules of Thumb
- One sentence of player intent per scene ("get the writ," "find the rope," "decide whether to speak").
- One planted hook per room.
- Threads run to natural exhaustion, then wait. Multiple open, pull one at a time.
- Abandoned threads leave scars (a name that isn’t there, a door that won’t open) rather than hard blocks.
- Forks are one-shot. Forced reconvergence at load-bearing events. Tree never grows past depth one.
- Post-scene dossier (period newspaper or historian’s note) doubles as canon documentation.
- Writing stays light; scene cards and historical notes do most of the work.
