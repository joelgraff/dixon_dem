# Dixon Scene Spine

Proposed fixed sequence for the historical adventure:

1. John Dixon settling / 1830 Ogee ferry (founding era) — prototype playthrough already sketched
2. Truesdell Bridge collapse, May 4 1873 (crisis state test)
3. Joseph Smith arrest, June 1843 (agency test — side with officers or warn Smith)
4. Reagan lifeguard years (coming-of-age)
5. Walgreens founding (civic growth)
6. Druggist murder, July 24 1968 (aftermath test) — Joseph Villiger, Benjamin Rippeon, Tom Wadsworth account
7. Billy Sunday revival, 1905 (load-bearing reconvergence)
8. Sterling-Dixon Interurban (transport/entry test)

Additional candidates: Black Hawk War / Fort Dixon (1832), Bloody Gulch (1885), Dixon High School milestones, notable persons (Louella Parsons, Rita Crundwell, etc.).

Historical sources live in the dixon_dem repo under docs/spec, docs/game-data/sources.yaml and archive/sierra-parser.
