# Lighthouse Keeper's Cottage — Scene Card

## Location
Lighthouse keeper's cottage, rocky Maine coast, 1840; the lighthouse stands beyond the west window.

## Era
1840, New England maritime.

## Sensory Details
- Salt-crusted window glass
- The lantern's wick hissing in its housing
- A smell of lamp oil and wet wool

## Interactive Objects
- Storm lantern on its shelf
- Keeper's logbook on the desk
- Brass telescope by the door

## Background Composition
Show the cottage interior from a fixed, straight-on adventure-game viewpoint. Place the west-facing door and brass telescope on the left, a desk with the logbook near the center, and the lantern on a wall shelf to the right. The spiral stair rises at the back-right toward the north. A salt-streaked window reveals the lighthouse beacon and a narrow strip of rocky Maine coast at night. Keep each interactive object distinct and unobstructed; leave the lower edge visually quiet for a future text interface.

## Entry and Exit Points
- Door to the west leads to the rocky path down to the dock
- Spiral stair to the north leads up to the lantern room

## Planted Hook
The logbook's last entry is dated three days ago and ends mid-sentence — "the light went out at midnight and I heard—" with no continuation.

## Object Aliases
- lantern: lamp, flame, light
- logbook: journal, diary, book
- telescope: spyglass, glass
- lighthouse: tower, beacon, light

## Contextual Objects
- lighthouse: The lighthouse stands beyond the west window, its beacon cutting through the salt haze.

16-color EGA palette with deliberate pixel dithering
