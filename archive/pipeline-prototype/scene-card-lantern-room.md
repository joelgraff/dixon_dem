# Lighthouse Lantern Room — Scene Card

## Location
Lantern room at the top of the lighthouse, rocky Maine coast, 1840; the keeper's cottage lies below, beyond the spiral stair.

## Era
1840, New England maritime.

## Sensory Details
- Salt-streaked panes rattling in their iron frames as wind presses against the tower
- The steady hiss of the beacon's oil lamp beneath the creak of metal
- Warm lamp oil mingled with cold, damp sea air

## Interactive Objects
- Beacon lamp burning behind a broad polished reflector at the center of the room
- Oil reservoir with a capped filling spout at the base of the beacon pedestal
- Hinged service panel standing slightly open on the front of the pedestal, its interior lost in shadow

## Background Composition
Show the lantern room interior from a fixed, straight-on adventure-game viewpoint, consistent with the cottage's bold silhouettes and hard pixel edges. Compose for a 320x200 screen. A broad oil-lamp reflector and its clearly visible flame dominate the center, mounted on a sturdy pedestal. Keep the capped oil reservoir visible on the pedestal's left side and the partly open service panel distinct on its front. Place the spiral stair opening at the lower-right, protected by a curved iron railing, with enough visible steps to make the downward exit unmistakable. Tall, salt-streaked window panes and dark iron mullions wrap around the rear of the room; show only a narrow band of dark sea and pale wave crests outside. Use deep blue and black for the sea and recesses, cool grays for iron and stone, brown for worn fittings, and restrained yellow and white highlights around the lamp and reflector. Leave a clear strip of stone floor across the foreground for the player to stand and move. Keep the lower edge visually quiet for a future text interface. No characters, readable lettering, or modern electrical equipment.

## Entry and Exit Points
- Spiral stair to the south leads to the keeper's cottage below; this is the downward return from the cottage's north/up exit

## Planted Hook
The service panel's latch is bent outward, and fresh scrape marks interrupt the old soot around its edge. The beacon is burning now, but something disturbed its housing recently. A portable storm lantern carried up from the cottage would illuminate the panel's shadowed interior without disturbing the beacon itself.
