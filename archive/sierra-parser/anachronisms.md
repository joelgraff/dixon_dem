# Anachronisms as Design

Anachronisms woven in as puzzles, not bugs. A player who notices a 1950s car parked outside an 1873 scene isn't seeing an error — they're seeing a clue that something's off in the timeline, which is exactly what a fork or crisis state would produce.

## Anachronism-hunting verb

"Examine" returns era-correct detail, but a sharp eye catches the wrong decade's streetlight and triggers a hidden thread. Makes observation itself a gameplay verb alongside the parser's noun-verb core.

## Rules

- Every anachronism must be traceable to a fork state or crisis phase. No random objects.
- The dossier can flag discovered anachronisms as bonus content.
- Risk: too many and the timeline feels broken; too few and the mechanic is invisible.
